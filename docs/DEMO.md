# SlugGrub demo run sheet (2 minutes)

Written 2026-11-15 for DevFest judging. Live at https://sluggrub.vercel.app.

## Before judging (5 minutes, once)

1. **Phone (the one you demo on):** Today → profile button (top right) → Settings → Start demo → tap again to confirm. Finish onboarding with a plan, your college and your hall. Demo mode is now on for this phone only: full leaderboards, college rivalry, an example plate on Today.
2. **Laptop on the projector:** open https://sluggrub.vercel.app/vote/screen. **Do not turn demo mode on here.** Demo mode lives in one device's browser, so a laptop in demo mode would show its own mock tally and never the judges' real votes. Tap "Next round" once so the round is fresh.
3. Test the live vote once: vote from a spare phone, and check the count goes up by exactly one on the projector.

## The run (about 2 minutes)

1. **The problem (15 s).** UCSC posts every hall's menu and nutrition labels, but nothing tells you what's actually good, what you just ate, or whether your Slug Points will last.
2. **Today (15 s).** Greeting, the #1 dish at your hall, Slug Points pace, your college's rank this week.
3. **Rank (20 s).** Rank → pick a few this-or-that matchups. Swipe back to change one. Open Leaderboard: halls, dishes, college rivalry.
4. **Live vote with the judges (25 s).** Point at the projector: "Scan this and vote." The tally moves as they vote. No install: it's a web app.
5. **Log a plate (25 s).** Blue food button → camera → "Use a demo plate". The beam runs while Gemini reads it, then it turns into the stats: dish, servings, calories from UCSC's own labels. Save it, and it shows on Today. (Have a real tray? "Take a photo" works too, if the food is on that hall's menu.)
6. **Ask SlugGrub (15 s).** Blue food button → sparkle → "High protein under 700 cal". The action line shows each real step, then the answer streams in with picks from what's open now.
7. **Budget (5 s).** Ring, "At this pace you finish with N left".
8. **Close (5 s).** Open https://sluggrub.vercel.app/dining-services: the same votes show dining services which dishes students love and which they keep passing on. That's the startup angle.

## If something goes wrong

- A hall is closed: the app moves to an open hall on its own; the demo plate follows it.
- Gemini is slow: the beam keeps running; the demo plate takes a few seconds.
- After judging: Settings → Leave demo removes the demo plates and turns the mock rankings off.

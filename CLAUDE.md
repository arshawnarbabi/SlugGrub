## Saving

The general `/save` files this project's records here: the shared plan and team decisions in `PLAN.md`; the visual system,
motion, effects and blur budget in `docs/DESIGN.md` (premium.md); locked choices in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (append-only); team build
conventions in `AGENTS.md`; open items in `~/.claude/open-items/sluggrub.json`. Checks: `npx tsc --noEmit -p .`, and the
premium-md drift check with `--root docs --src ../src`.
